# Mobile Marketing Performance Report
## Competency Unit 5: Mobile Marketing Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Performance Report evaluates the effectiveness of WhatsApp Business as a mobile marketing channel for Superbowl Lipis over the review period. It assesses campaign engagement, conversion outcomes, and customer growth metrics, and recommends strategic adjustments for the next planning cycle.

---

## 2. Review Period: Q1–Q2 2025 (January–May)

## 3. Performance Summary

| Metric | Target | Actual | Status |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Total WhatsApp contacts | 150 | 112 | ⚠️ Below |
| Broadcast campaigns sent | 6 | 5 | ⚠️ Below |
| Average message open rate | ≥ 70% | 84% | ✅ Exceeded |
| Orders attributed to WhatsApp | 30/month | 22/month | ⚠️ Below |
| WhatsApp conversion rate | ≥ 30% | 38% | ✅ Exceeded |
| Average response time | < 15 min | 28 min | ❌ Below |

---

## 5. Key Findings

| Finding | Insight |
|---------|---------|
| VIP segment has highest conversion | Targeted messages to repeat customers convert 2.5x more than general broadcasts |
| Response time of 28 min hurts conversion | Enquiries left >15 minutes are 40% less likely to convert |
| List growth below target | Insufficient CTAs on TikTok and social channels pointing to WhatsApp |
| Seasonal campaigns outperform generic ones | Open rates 10–15% higher for Raya/Merdeka-themed messages |

---

## 6. Recommendations

| Recommendation | Priority | Owner |
|---------------|----------|-------|
| Set up WhatsApp quick reply templates to reduce response time | High | Zuriel |
| Add "WhatsApp us" CTA to every TikTok LIVE session | High | Zuriel |
| Increase VIP segment broadcasts to 2x/month | Medium | Zuriel |
| Train 1 staff member on WhatsApp customer service | Medium | Zuriel |
| Add WhatsApp QR code to product packaging | Medium | Zuriel + Ops |
