# Mobile Marketing Campaign Plan (WhatsApp)
## Competency Unit 5: Mobile Marketing Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Mobile Marketing Campaign Plan establishes the strategic framework for using WhatsApp Business as the primary mobile marketing channel for Superbowl Lipis (SBL Co). The plan defines audience segmentation, message scheduling, campaign calendar coordination, and performance measurement to deliver measurable sales and customer retention outcomes through mobile-first direct communication.

This document elevates WhatsApp usage from informal customer messaging (operational) to a structured, planned marketing channel with deliberate audience segmentation and campaign coordination (managerial).

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives

| Objective | KPI | Target (per month) |
|-----------|-----|--------------------|
| Drive repeat purchases | Repeat order rate via WhatsApp | 20% of monthly orders |
| Grow broadcast list | Total opted-in contacts | +50 new contacts/month |
| Promote campaigns effectively | Campaign message open rate | ≥ 70% |
| Reduce customer service response time | Average first response time | < 15 minutes |
| Convert enquiries to orders | WhatsApp conversion rate | ≥ 30% |

## 6. Message Content Framework

All campaign messages follow this structure:

```
[Greeting / Seasonal tie-in]
[Product/offer announcement — 1 sentence]
[Key benefit — 1 sentence]
[Call to action: Order now / Reply YES / Click link]
[Contact / Payment info]
```

**Example (Merdeka Sale broadcast):**
> Selamat Hari Merdeka! 🇲🇧 Sempena sambutan Merdeka, kami tawarkan DISKAUN 31% untuk semua produk pilihan hari ini sahaja. Stok terhad — balas “ORDER” sekarang atau layari TikTok Shop kami. Terima kasih kerana menyokong perniagaan tempatan!
