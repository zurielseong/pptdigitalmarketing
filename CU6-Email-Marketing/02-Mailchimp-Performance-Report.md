# Email Marketing Performance Report (Mailchimp)
## Competency Unit 6: Email Marketing Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This report evaluates the performance of email marketing campaigns conducted via Mailchimp for TVET Lipis over the review period. It compares results against the objectives in the Email Marketing Campaign Plan, identifies factors driving strong and weak performance, and recommends optimisations for the next planning cycle.

---

## 6. Key Findings

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| Welcome sequence has highest open rate (51%) | Automation at point of interest capture is most effective |
| CTR drops in later campaign emails | Content relevance decreases over time — need fresher topics |
| "Last call" campaigns have highest conversion | Urgency messaging drives action — schedule 2 per intake |
| List still small (99 contacts) | List building needs acceleration in Q2 |
