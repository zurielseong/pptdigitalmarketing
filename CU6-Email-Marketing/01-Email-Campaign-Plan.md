# Email Marketing Campaign Plan
## Competency Unit 6: Email Marketing Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Email Marketing Campaign Plan defines the strategic use of Mailchimp as the primary email marketing platform for TVET Lipis (tvetlipis.my). It establishes the audience segmentation framework, campaign calendar, automation sequences, and performance governance process to support student enrolment objectives.

Email marketing is used as a nurture channel to convert prospective students who have shown interest (through website visits, event sign-ups, or enquiries) into enrolled students, and to maintain engagement with current students and alumni.

---

## 2. Strategic Objectives

| Objective | KPI | 3-Month Target | 6-Month Target |
|-----------|-----|----------------|----------------|
| Build a qualified email list | Total subscribers | 200 | 500 |
| Nurture prospective students | Lead-to-enrolment conversion (email-attributed) | 5/intake | 15/intake |
| Maintain open rate | Average open rate | ≥ 25% | ≥ 30% |
| Drive website traffic | Click-through rate (CTR) | ≥ 5% | ≥ 8% |
| Reduce unsubscribe rate | Unsubscribe rate per campaign | ≤ 1% | ≤ 0.5% |

---

## 3. Audience Segmentation

| Segment | Tag in Mailchimp | Source | Content Focus |
|---------|-----------------|--------|---------------|
| Prospective students (new leads) | `prospect` | Website enquiry form, event sign-up | Programme info, testimonials, intake dates |
| Applied / Awaiting confirmation | `applied` | CRM handover from TVET Lipis CRM | Application status, document checklist |
| Enrolled students (current) | `enrolled` | Post-enrolment data entry | Timetables, announcements, events |
| Alumni | `alumni` | Graduation records | Career tips, referral programme, events |
| Staff (internal) | `staff` | Manual | Internal announcements |

## 4. Email Automation Sequences

| Email # | Timing | Subject | Content |
|---------|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | On enrolment | Selamat Datang ke TVET Lipis! | Welcome, what to expect, orientation date |
