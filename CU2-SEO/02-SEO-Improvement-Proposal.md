# SEO Improvement Proposal
## Competency Unit 2: Search Engine Optimisation Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This SEO Improvement Proposal outlines a structured 6-month strategy to increase organic search visibility for TVET Lipis (tvetlipis.my). Building on the findings of the SEO Channel Assessment (TVETLIPIS-CU2-SEO-001), this proposal defines the content roadmap, technical optimisation priorities, off-page authority building plan, and the resource allocation required to execute the strategy effectively.

The strategic objective is to rank tvetlipis.my on the first page of Google for a minimum of 10 high-intent local keywords within 6 months, thereby generating a measurable increase in organic enrolment enquiries.

### 4.2 Platform Limitation Workarounds

Since Canva does not support Schema.org markup or a native blog, the following workarounds are proposed:
- **FAQ content** embedded as visible text sections on programme pages (captures featured snippet opportunities)
- **Google Sites** or a free WordPress subdomain as a supplementary content blog, cross-linked to tvetlipis.my
- **Google Business Profile posts** as a secondary content publication channel for time-sensitive updates (intake dates, events)
