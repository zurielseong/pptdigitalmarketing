# E-Commerce Marketing Strategy
## Competency Unit 4: E-Commerce Marketing Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This E-Commerce Marketing Strategy document establishes the planning framework for Superbowl Lipis (SBL Co) to leverage TikTok Shop and other e-commerce channels as a primary revenue driver. This plan moves beyond basic product listing (operational) to define a coordinated approach to product strategy, promotional calendar, team coordination, and performance optimisation that demonstrates managerial competence at Level 4.

The strategy covers catalogue management, platform selection, affiliate/creator coordination, and budget allocation across the e-commerce marketing function.

## 4. Platform Strategy

### 4.1 TikTok Shop (Primary)

| Feature | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Shop setup** | Complete seller verification, configure shipping, set up product categories |
| **Product listings** | Professional photos, keyword-optimised titles and descriptions in BM/EN |
| **TikTok LIVE selling** | 2x per week; demo products, limited-time offers, Q&A |
| **Short video content** | 3–5 product-linked videos/week driving traffic to TikTok Shop |
| **TikTok Shop Ads** | Product Shopping Ads targeted at Pahang + national audience |
| **Affiliate programme** | Recruit local micro-influencers and TikTok creators to promote products on commission |

### 4.2 Facebook Marketplace (Secondary)

| Feature | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Listings** | Mirror top-selling TikTok Shop products |
| **Targeting** | Local Kuala Lipis / Pahang buyers |
| **Response time** | < 1 hour during business hours |

## 6. TikTok LIVE Strategy

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Schedule** | Tuesday 8pm & Saturday 2pm |
| **Duration** | 60–90 minutes per session |
| **Format** | Product demo → flash deal announcement → Q&A → CTA to shop |
| **Host** | Zuriel Seong (primary) / Trained staff (backup) |
| **Engagement tactics** | Games, giveaways, countdown timers, pinned comments |
| **Flash deal** | Min. 1 exclusive LIVE-only discount per session (10–20% off) |
| **KPI** | 500 concurrent viewers by Month 3; RM 500 GMV per LIVE session |
