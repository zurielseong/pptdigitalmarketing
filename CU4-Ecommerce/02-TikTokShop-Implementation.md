## 3. Lessons Learned & Optimisations Applied

| Issue Encountered | Resolution | Impact |
|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| LIVE sessions in daytime had low viewership | Shifted to 8pm weekday schedule | Viewer count +180% |
| Product titles not appearing in search | Added Malay keywords in brackets | Impressions +65% |
| First TikTok Ads campaign had high CPM | Narrowed audience to Pahang + interest targeting | CPM reduced 40% |
| Low affiliate content quality | Created written brief + sample talking points | Content quality improved |
