### 6.6 Conversion Funnel Analysis

```
Ad impressions:   51,230  (100%)
       ↓
Link clicks:       1,378  (2.7% of impressions)
       ↓
Sessions:          1,241  (90.1% click-to-session, 9.9% drop-off)
       ↓
Add to cart:         312  (25.1% of sessions)
       ↓
Reached checkout:    198  (63.5% of add-to-cart)
       ↓
Orders placed:        78  (39.4% checkout completion rate)
       ↓
GMV:           RM 4,512
```

**Key funnel findings:**
- Click-to-session drop (9.9%) is acceptable — some mobile users bounce immediately
- Cart-to-checkout drop (36.5%) is the biggest loss point — needs improvement
- Checkout completion (39.4%) is below industry average (60–70%)
- **Priority fix:** Simplify checkout process; add "DM to order" option for WhatsApp-preferring customers

## WA7 — Optimisation of E-Commerce Marketing Activities

### 7.1 Issues Identified and Optimisation Actions Taken

| # | Issue | Root Cause | Action Taken | Outcome |
|---|-------|------------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | CTR below 2.5% in Week 1 (AD-01) | Single image ad with generic copy | Switched to carousel format (AD-02) by Week 2 | CTR improved from 2.1% → 5.8% |
| 2 | High CPO on Male 35–44 segment (RM 35.60) | Outside core brand demographic | Excluded 35–44 male from AS-01 and AS-02 from Feb onwards | CPO reduced to RM 15.50 average |
| 3 | Low cart-to-checkout rate (36.5%) | Checkout via WhatsApp creates friction | Added clearer WhatsApp order button in cart; improved CTA copy | Checkout rate improved to 48% in May |
| 4 | Email subscriber target missed (184 vs 200) | Newsletter form not prominent enough | Moved newsletter CTA higher on homepage; added exit-intent prompt | +31 subscribers in May alone |
| 5 | Tote bag underperforming (7 units only) | No compelling visual content; unclear use case | Created "market bag" lifestyle content; added to sticker bundle offer | Units sold doubled to 14 in Apr–May combined |
| 6 | May post-Raya slowdown | Seasonal demand dip | Shifted budget fully to retargeting (AS-03); paused cold audiences | Maintained positive ROAS (2.37x) despite low season |

### 7.2 A/B Test Results

| Test | Variable A | Variable B | Winner | Lift |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------|------|
| Ad format test (Jan) | Single image | Carousel | Carousel | +38% CTR |
| CTA copy test (Feb) | "Shop Now" | "Buy Now" | "Shop Now" | +12% CTR |

| Homepage hero CTA (Apr) | "Shop Now" button | "Shop Now" + "View Collections" dual CTA | Dual CTA | +18% click-through to products |

### Key Decisions Made by Manager (Zuriel Seong)
