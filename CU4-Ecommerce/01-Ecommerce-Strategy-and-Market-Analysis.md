## 1. Strategic Purpose

This document records the strategic planning process behind Superbowl Lipis's entry into direct-to-consumer (D2C) e-commerce. Superbowl Lipis (SBL Co) is a lifestyle and merchandise brand based in Kuala Lipis, Pahang. Prior to 2025, all merchandise sales were conducted through physical walk-ins and informal WhatsApp orders — there was no centralised online sales channel, no inventory tracking, and no ability to run paid traffic campaigns against a conversion destination.

The e-commerce initiative was commissioned by management in Q4 2024 to:
- Create a scalable sales channel independent of physical location
- Enable Meta Ads and TikTok Ads campaigns with a trackable landing destination
- Build customer data infrastructure (email capture, order history)
- Establish the brand's digital retail presence ahead of planned expansion

## WA1 — E-Commerce Market and Competition Analysis

### 1.1 Malaysian E-Commerce Market Context

| Indicator | 2024 Data | Source |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Malaysia e-commerce market size | USD 10.1 billion | Statista 2024 |
| YoY growth rate | 14.2% | MDEC Report |
| Mobile commerce share | 72% of all transactions | Google APAC |
| Most popular payment method | FPX (47%), e-wallet (38%) | BNM 2024 |
| Average basket size (fashion/lifestyle) | RM 68–95 | Shopee Malaysia data |
| Social commerce penetration | 61% of online shoppers | Nielsen Malaysia |

**Key insight:** The Malaysian consumer increasingly discovers products via social media but completes purchases via a trusted storefront — either marketplace (Shopee/Lazada) or branded D2C. For a brand like SBL Co with strong local identity, D2C builds brand equity that marketplace listings cannot.

### 1.2 Platform Comparison & Selection Rationale

| Platform | Setup Cost | Monthly Fee | Transaction Fee | Brand Control | Data Ownership | Ad Integration |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| Shopify | RM 0 | RM 120–500 | 0.5–2% | High | Full | Excellent |
| Shopee | RM 0 | RM 0 | 3–5% per sale | Low | None | Limited |
| Lazada | RM 0 | RM 0 | 4–6% per sale | Low | None | Limited |
| TikTok Shop | RM 0 | RM 0 | 1–5% per sale | Medium | Partial | TikTok only |
| **Custom D2C Store** | **RM 0** | **RM 0** | **0%** | **Full** | **Full** | **All platforms** |
| WooCommerce | RM 0 | RM 15–80 (hosting) | 0–2% | Full | Full | Good |

**Decision:** Custom-built D2C storefront deployed on GitHub Pages (zero cost), with full brand control and no commission fees. This approach is optimal for a brand at SBL Co's stage: zero overhead during validation, full creative freedom, and the ability to direct all paid media (Meta, TikTok) to a single owned URL.

### 1.3 Competitor Analysis

| Competitor | Platform | Strengths | Weaknesses | SBL Opportunity |
|-----------|----------|-----------|------------|------------------|
| Kapas Club (Sabah merch) | Shopify | Strong branding, loyal fanbase | Limited Pahang relevance | Own the Pahang/Lipis identity niche |
| KL Drip (streetwear) | Custom + Shopee | Wide reach | Generic — no local identity | Hyper-local positioning |
| Local football club merch | WhatsApp/manual | Existing community | No e-commerce, no tracking | Better UX + paid traffic |
| Instagramable Brands | Instagram Shop | Aesthetic content | High CPM, low conversion | Better landing page experience |

**Gap identified:** No Pahang-based lifestyle brand currently operates a branded D2C store with active paid media campaigns. SBL Co has first-mover advantage in this specific niche.

### 2.2 E-Commerce Marketing Funnel

```
AWARENESS
  Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) → reach local + diaspora audience
  TikTok organic content → product showcase reels
  ↓
CONSIDERATION
  Store homepage → collections → product pages
  Social proof (reviews, UGC reposts)
  ↓
CONVERSION
  Add to cart → promo code SBL10 (10% off) → checkout
  Free shipping threshold RM 100 (AOV booster)
  ↓
RETENTION
  Newsletter (post-purchase sequence)
  WhatsApp broadcast (repeat buyers)
  Seasonal promotions (Raya, Merdeka, 11.11)
```
