# Online Reputation Management Plan
## Competency Unit 7: Online Reputation Management Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Reputation Management Plan establishes the processes, responsibilities, escalation protocols, and monitoring systems required to protect and actively improve the online reputation of TVET Lipis and Superbowl Lipis (SBL Co). It transforms ad hoc reputation management into a structured, proactive function managed with clear accountability.

A strong online reputation directly supports enrolment conversion (TVET Lipis) and purchase conversion (SBL Co), making this a strategic business priority.

## 4. Review Response Protocol

### Positive Reviews

| Timing | Template Approach |
|--------|------------------|
| Within 24 hours | Thank the reviewer by name, mention a specific detail they raised, invite them to return/refer a friend |

### Negative Reviews

| Step | Action | Timing |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Acknowledge the review publicly with empathy | < 2 hours |
| 2 | Apologise without admitting specific fault | < 2 hours |
| 3 | Invite offline resolution (DM / phone call) | < 2 hours |
| 4 | Resolve issue with customer | < 24 hours |
| 5 | Request review update if resolved | After resolution |
| 6 | Log incident in reputation tracker | Same day |

**Response Template (Negative):**
> "Terima kasih atas maklum balas anda. Kami memohon maaf atas pengalaman yang kurang memuaskan ini. Sila hubungi kami terus di [contact] supaya kami dapat menyelesaikan isu ini dengan segera."

---

## 5. Crisis Response Protocol

A reputation crisis is defined as: any single incident receiving 3+ negative public mentions within 24 hours, or any viral negative content with 1,000+ views.

| Level | Trigger | Response | Owner |
|-------|---------|----------|-------|
| Level 1 (Minor) | 1 negative review or comment | Standard response protocol | Staff / Zuriel |
| Level 2 (Moderate) | 3+ negatives in 24 hrs, or complaint going viral | Zuriel coordinates response; notify supervisor | Zuriel + Supervisor |
| Level 3 (Major) | Media coverage, significant reputational damage | Immediate escalation to management; consider public statement | Manager + Zuriel |

## 7. Monthly Reputation Report

A monthly reputation summary will be compiled and submitted to the supervisor covering:
1. Total reviews received (by platform)
2. Average rating (by platform)
3. Review response rate
4. Negative incidents and resolutions
5. Sentiment trend (positive / neutral / negative %)
6. Recommended actions for next month
