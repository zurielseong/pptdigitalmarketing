# Online Reputation Assessment
## Competency Unit 7: Online Reputation Management Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Online Reputation Assessment evaluates the current digital reputation of Superbowl Lipis (SBL Co) and TVET Lipis (tvetlipis.my) across all major online platforms. The assessment identifies reputation risks, sentiment trends, and gaps in reputation management processes, providing the factual foundation for the Reputation Management Plan.

For an educational institution (TVET Lipis) and a consumer retail/F&B business (SBL Co), online reputation is a direct driver of enrolment and sales decisions. Prospective students and customers routinely check reviews before making contact.
