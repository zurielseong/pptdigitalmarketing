## 5. Root Cause Analysis (Programme Discovery Underperformance)

| Issue | Root Cause | Evidence |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| High CPC | Broad match keywords driving up bids | Search term report showed irrelevant queries |
| Low conversion rate | Landing page lacked clear call-to-action | Heatmap analysis — users leaving at programme page |
| Low CTR | Ad headlines not matching user search intent | Low headline performance scores in RSA report |

## 7. Optimisation Recommendations

| Priority | Recommendation | Expected Impact | Timeline |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|----------|
| 1 (Critical) | Switch broad match to phrase/exact match for discovery campaign | Reduce wasted spend 30–40% | Immediate |
| 2 (High) | Rebuild programme landing pages with clear enquiry CTA above fold | Improve CVR from 8.7% to 12%+ | Month 1 |
| 3 (High) | Add 25 negative keywords from search term report | Reduce irrelevant clicks | Immediate |
| 4 (Medium) | Test 3 new headline variations focused on "yuran" and "JPK" | Improve CTR to 9%+ | Month 1 |
| 5 (Medium) | Enable call tracking extension | Capture phone leads not tracked | Month 1 |
| 6 (Low) | Activate seasonal campaign for next intake (July 2025) | Capture high-intent intake traffic | Month 2 |
