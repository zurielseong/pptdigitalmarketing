# Search Engine Marketing (SEM) Campaign Plan
## Competency Unit 3: Search Engine Marketing Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This SEM Campaign Plan establishes the strategic framework for running paid search advertising campaigns via Google Ads for TVET Lipis (tvetlipis.my). The plan supports the institution's enrolment targets by placing TVET Lipis prominently in front of prospective students actively searching for vocational education programmes in Pahang and Malaysia.

Unlike organic SEO, which requires 3–6 months to yield results, SEM provides immediate visibility during critical intake periods. This plan coordinates the budget, campaign structure, keyword bidding strategy, ad creative direction, and performance optimisation processes across intake cycles.

---

## 2. Campaign Objectives

| Objective | Metric | Target (per intake cycle) |
|-----------|--------|---------------------------|
| Drive qualified traffic to programme pages | Clicks | 300 clicks/month |
| Generate enrolment enquiries | Leads (form fills + calls) | 30 leads/month |
| Maximise budget efficiency | Cost Per Lead (CPL) | ≤ RM 15 |
| Maintain visibility for brand queries | Impression share | ≥ 60% |
| Optimise conversion rate | Landing page CVR | ≥ 10% |

---

## 3. Target Audience Definition

| Segment | Description | Geography |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Primary** | SPM school leavers aged 17–22 seeking vocational career paths | Kuala Lipis, Pahang |
| **Secondary** | Working adults aged 23–35 seeking upskilling/career change | Pahang state-wide |
| **Tertiary** | Parents researching programmes for children | Pahang state-wide |

**Google Ads Targeting Settings:**
- Location: Pahang state (radius: 100km from Kuala Lipis)
- Language: Bahasa Malaysia + English
- Device: All (mobile bidding adjustment: +20%)
- Ad schedule: 8am–9pm (Mon–Sun, peak during intake periods)

- 24 Bulan ke Kerjaya Impian

- Belajar di Kuala Lipis

## 6. Landing Page Requirements

Each campaign should direct traffic to a dedicated, optimised landing page:

| Campaign | Landing Page | Required Elements |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| Brand | tvetlipis.my (Home) | Clear CTA, programme list, contact |
| Programme Discovery | /programmes or specific course page | Course details, fees, entry req., form |
| Intake Seasonal | /daftar-sekarang (intake-specific) | Urgency messaging, short form, phone |

**Conversion Actions to Track:**
1. Contact form submission
2. Phone call (call tracking number)
3. WhatsApp button click

## 7. Negative Keyword List

To prevent wasted spend on irrelevant queries:

| Category | Negative Keywords |
|----------|------------------|
| Competitor brand | GIATMARA, ILP, politeknik, universiti |
| Employment (not study) | kerja, jawatan, job, vacancy, loker |
| Free/pirated content | percuma, free download, bahan pdf |
| Unrelated courses | sijil pelajaran, SPM, UPSR |
