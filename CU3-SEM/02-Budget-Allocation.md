## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Budget Allocation and Matching Report defines the approved financial resources allocated to TVET Lipis Google Ads campaigns, maps each budget line to specific campaign objectives, and establishes the governance process for expenditure approval and monitoring. ## 5. Performance-Linked Budget Scaling Rules

| Condition | Action | Approval Required |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| CPL < RM 10 for 2 consecutive weeks | Increase campaign budget by 20% | Supervisor |
| CPL > RM 30 for 1 week | Pause campaign, review keywords | Self (Zuriel) |
| CTR < 2% on any ad group for 2 weeks | Rewrite ad copy | Self (Zuriel) |
| Monthly budget exhausted before month-end | Request budget supplement | Manager |
| Intake period approaching (2 weeks prior) | Activate seasonal campaign | Supervisor |

## 6. Cost Comparison: SEM vs. Traditional

| Channel | Cost | Leads Generated | CPL |
|---------|------|----------------|-----|

| Flyer distribution (est.) | RM 500/run | ~10 leads | RM 50 |
| Radio spot (RTM Pahang, est.) | RM 2,000/month | ~15 leads | RM 133 |
| Facebook Ads (est.) | RM 500/month | ~20 leads | RM 25 |

**Conclusion:** Google Ads SEM delivers the lowest CPL among paid channels when intent-based keywords are properly targeted, justifying the budget allocation above.
