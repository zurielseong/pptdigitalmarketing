## 4. Creative Performance Analysis

### 4.1 Creative Fatigue Diagnosis

| Creative Name | CTR (all) | Status | Action |
|--------------|-----------|--------|--------|
| PAKK Copywriting Lama | 1.23% | ❌ Fatigued | Phase out — high spend, declining CTR |
| Copywriting SPM PAKK Lama | **2.01%** | ✅ Star performer | Scale — allocate 40% of copy budget |
| Fara PAKK VS PRA (video) | 1.87% | ✅ Strong | Maintain — good video engagement |
| Full Time Parents 2026 (lead ad) | 1.45% | ✅ Performing | Continue — strong lead volume |
| Generic programme image | 0.89% | ❌ Underperforming | Pause immediately |

**Key Finding:** The "SPM" keyword in copywriting resonates significantly more (CTR 2.01%) than generic "PAKK" framing (1.23%). This suggests the audience responds to pain-point language ("anak SPM") more than programme-name language.
