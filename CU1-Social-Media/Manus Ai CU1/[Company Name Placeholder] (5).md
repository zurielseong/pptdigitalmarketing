## Campaign Optimization Strategy Report

**1. Purpose**
This report outlines the strategic adjustments and optimizations required to improve the efficiency and performance of the social media marketing campaigns based on real-time data analysis.

**2. Data Analysis**
A review of the "Full Time Parents 2026" campaign highlights several optimization opportunities:

### 2.1 Performance Findings
*   **Creative Fatigue**: The "PAKK Copywriting Lama" ad has high spend but a lower **CTR (all)** compared to newer creatives like "Copywriting SPM PAKK Lama" (**1.23% vs 2.01%**).
*   **Placement Efficiency**: **Facebook News Feed** accounts for **85%** of conversions, while other placements show higher costs per lead.
*   **Demographic Shift**: Engagement is increasing among the **25-34** age group, though the **35-44** group remains the volume leader.

### 2.2 Optimization Metrics
| Current Metric | Target Metric | Gap Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CTR (all)**: 1.33% | 1.80% | Creative refresh needed |
| **Cost per result**: RM 4.61 | RM 4.00 | Targeting refinement required |
| **Frequency**: 2.6 | < 2.0 | Audience expansion required |

**3. Strategic Proposal**
Based on the findings, we recommend the following optimization actions:
1.  **Creative Refresh**: Phase out low-CTR creatives and scale the "Copywriting SPM" series.
2.  **Placement Optimization**: Reallocate budget from Audience Network to Facebook and Instagram News Feeds to maximize conversion efficiency.
3.  **Audience Expansion**: Introduce a "Lookalike Audience" based on the top 111 leads generated to find similar high-intent Accounts Center accounts.
4.  **Bid Strategy**: Implement "Cost Cap" bidding at RM 4.50 to maintain a stable **Cost per result**.
