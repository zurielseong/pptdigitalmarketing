## Social Media Channel Setup Proposal

**1. Purpose**
The strategic objective of this proposal is to select and justify the most effective social media channels for the upcoming marketing campaign based on historical performance data and audience behavioral patterns from the Meta Ads account. This ensures that resource allocation is optimized for maximum engagement and conversion.

**2. Data Analysis**
Based on the Meta Ads account (act_494800392480239) historical data from the last 90 days:

### 2.1 Audience Persona Profile
*   **Demographics**: The primary audience consists of Accounts Center accounts aged **25-44**, with the **35-44** age group showing the highest volume of impressions (**439,773**) and a strong **CTR (all)** of **1.45%**.
*   **Gender**: Female Accounts Center accounts represent the dominant segment, with **238,148** impressions and a superior **CTR (all)** of **1.61%**, compared to male Accounts Center accounts.
*   **Interests & Behaviors**: The data indicates a high interest in educational and parenting content, as evidenced by the performance of campaigns like "Full Time Parents 2026".

### 2.2 Traffic Data Insight Analysis
| Channel/Platform | Impressions | Clicks (all) | CTR (all) | Spend (MYR) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Facebook** | 434,211 | 7,382 | 1.70% | RM 3,099.27 |
| **Instagram** | 246,360 | 2,888 | 1.17% | RM 1,653.60 |
| **Audience Network** | 3,249 | 51 | 1.57% | RM 21.21 |

**Findings**:
*   **Facebook** is the highest-performing channel in terms of both volume and efficiency, yielding the highest **CTR (all)** of **1.70%**.
*   **Instagram** serves as a strong secondary channel, particularly for visual engagement, though with a lower **CTR (all)** compared to Facebook.
*   **Mobile Engagement**: **99%** of traffic originates from mobile devices (Android and iPhone), highlighting the need for mobile-first content implementation.

**3. Strategic Proposal**
Based on the analysis, we recommend a multi-channel strategy prioritizing **Facebook** and **Instagram**:
*   **Primary Channel (Facebook)**: Focus on community-building and long-form informational content (e.g., "PAKK Copywriting") to drive leads.
*   **Secondary Channel (Instagram)**: Utilize high-quality visual content and Reels to capture the attention of the younger demographic within our target persona (25-34).
*   **Implementation Strategy**: Adopt a mobile-first design approach for all creative assets to align with the dominant device usage patterns.
