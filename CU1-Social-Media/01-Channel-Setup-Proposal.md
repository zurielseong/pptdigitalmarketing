## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Social Media Channel Setup Proposal establishes the strategic rationale for selecting and prioritising social media platforms for TVET Lipis (tvetlipis.my) student recruitment campaigns. The selection is based on 90 days of verified performance data from Meta Ads account **act_494800392480239**, ensuring that budget and manpower are allocated to the channels that demonstrably deliver the highest quality leads at the lowest cost.

This document moves beyond platform selection (operational) to define the **governance framework** — who manages each channel, how performance is monitored, and when reallocation decisions are triggered — reflecting the managerial competency required at Level 4.

## 5. Channel Strategy & Selection

### 5.1 Recommended Channel Mix

| Channel | Role | Content Focus | Budget Share |
|---------|------|---------------|-------------|
| **Facebook (Primary)** | Lead generation + Community | Long-form copywriting, video, lead ads | 60% |
| **Instagram (Secondary)** | Brand awareness + Younger audience | Reels, carousel, Stories | 30% |
| **TikTok (Test)** | Organic reach + Younger audience | Short-form video, student testimonials | 10% |

### 5.2 Platform-Specific Strategy

**Facebook:**
- Proven highest CTR (1.70%) and highest lead volume
- Focus: Lead Ad campaigns ("Full Time Parents 2026" model), PAKK copywriting series
- Placement: Mobile News Feed (primary), Stories (secondary)
- Best posting times: 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM (verified from historical data)

**Instagram:**
- Strong visual engagement, especially Reels format
- Focus: Programme highlight videos, student day-in-life content, testimonials
- Target: Age 25–34 segment (secondary persona)
- Best posting times: 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

**TikTok:**
- Organic-first approach to test content resonance before committing paid budget
- Focus: Myth-busting content ("Common Myths about TVET"), student success stories
- Budget: RM 300/month test budget in Month 3 if organic shows traction

## 8. Channel Governance & Performance Triggers

| Trigger Condition | Action | Responsible |
|-------------------|--------|-------------|
| Platform CTR drops below 0.90% for 2 consecutive weeks | Pause and review creatives | Zuriel |
| Cost per lead exceeds RM 7.00 | Reallocate budget to better-performing channel | Zuriel + Supervisor |
| Instagram CTR exceeds Facebook CTR | Shift 10% budget from Facebook to Instagram | Zuriel (with approval) |
| TikTok organic post reaches 10,000 views | Activate TikTok paid budget | Zuriel |
| Any channel negative comment volume increases >50% | Trigger reputation protocol (CU7) | Zuriel |
