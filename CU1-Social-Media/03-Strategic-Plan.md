## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Campaign Strategic Plan defines the managerial framework for the **"2026 Full-Time Intake" social media marketing campaign** for TVET Lipis. It establishes SMART campaign objectives, defines the audience targeting strategy, outlines the two-tier funnel structure, and allocates budget and human resources across all campaign phases.

This document is the master reference that governs all tactical decisions made in CU1 sub-documents (Channel Setup, Content Calendar, Implementation Summary, Paid Ad Proposal, and Optimization Report). Any deviation from this plan requires written supervisor approval.

## 3. SMART Objectives

Objectives are set based on historical campaign performance from act_494800392480239 (Cost per lead: RM 4.61; CTR: 1.33%–1.70%):

| Objective | S | M | A | R | T |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead Generation** | Generate qualified leads for 2026 intake | Tracked via Meta Lead Forms + Pixel conversions | Achievable at RM 5.00 target CPL with RM 2,500 budget | Directly supports enrolment KPI | 90-day campaign (Jul–Sep 2025) |
| **Volume Target** | 500 qualified leads total | Leads dashboard in Meta Ads Manager | Based on historical RM 4.61 CPL with 8.5% buffer | Aligns with intake capacity of 4 programmes | By 30 September 2025 |
| **Awareness** | Reach 1,000,000 total impressions | Impressions metric in Ads Manager | Facebook historically delivers 434K+/90 days at current spend | Supports top-of-funnel brand building | 90-day window |
| **Engagement** | Achieve avg CTR ≥ 1.50% across all campaigns | CTR (all) in Ads Manager | Current baseline 1.33%–1.70%; target is mid-range | Indicates creative relevance to audience | Measured monthly |
| **Cost Efficiency** | Maintain CPL ≤ RM 5.00 | Cost per result in Ads Manager | Historical CPL RM 4.61; target includes 8.5% buffer | Ensures campaign ROI | Measured weekly |

## 5. Campaign Funnel Structure

```
TOP OF FUNNEL (Awareness)
  Broad targeting | Video content | Educational posts
  Objective: Reach + Video Views
  Budget: 30% of total
           ↓
MIDDLE OF FUNNEL (Consideration)
  Interest-based targeting | Programme highlight content | Testimonials
  Objective: Engagement + Link Clicks
  Budget: 30% of total
           ↓
BOTTOM OF FUNNEL (Conversion)
  Retargeting (pixel + video viewers) | Lead Form ads | Urgency copy
  Objective: Lead Generation
  Budget: 40% of total
```
