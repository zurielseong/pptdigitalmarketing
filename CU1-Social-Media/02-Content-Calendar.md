# Social Media Marketing Content Calendar
## Competency Unit 1: Social Media Marketing Campaign Plan
**M731-001-4:2021 — Digital Marketing Planning & Implementation (Level 4)**

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

This Content Calendar establishes a structured, three-month content publishing schedule for TVET Lipis across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. It coordinates content creation, approval, scheduling, and publication to ensure consistent brand messaging and optimal audience engagement throughout the student recruitment cycle.

This document operates at a **managerial level** — it does not just list posts, but defines the content strategy pillars, assigns ownership for each content type, sets publication governance (approval before posting), and links every content decision to a measurable business objective.
