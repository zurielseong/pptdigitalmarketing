## 5. Ad Creative Plan

### 5.1 Creative Variants per Ad Set

Each ad set will run a minimum of **2 creative variants** for A/B testing:

| Ad Set | Variant A (Control) | Variant B (Test) | Test Hypothesis |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Parents-TOF | Long-form PAKK copywriting (proven) | 60-sec video testimonial | Does video outperform copy at TOF? |
| YoungAdults-TOF | Reel — career outcomes | Carousel — 5 programme options | Does carousel drive more clicks? |
| PAKK-Parents-MOF | "Copywriting SPM PAKK Lama" style | New "Limited Seats" urgency copy | Does urgency improve MOF CTR? |
| Pixel-Retarget-BOF | Direct Lead Form CTA | Soft CTA with testimonial first | Does social proof increase BOF CVR? |

### 5.2 Creative Rotation Policy

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Creative frequency reaches 2.5 | Introduce new variant; retire lowest CTR |
| CTR drops below 1.00% for 7 days | Pause creative; replace with new variant |
| A variant CTR exceeds B by 30%+ for 7 days | Scale A; retire B; test new B |
